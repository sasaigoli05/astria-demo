Leaving incarceration should never mean leaving care.

Astria is a care-navigation platform that follows a person across the boundaries between corrections, coverage, and community care — and keeps checking that someone is still holding on.

The platform

Four pieces, one path

Onboarding

Argo

An AI system with a human in the loop. Give it a fax, a scan, a spreadsheet — it extracts what matters, assembles the Medicaid application, and produces a usable starting record.
Continuity

The Astria ID

An encrypted identifier that resolves a booking number, a Medicaid ID, and clinic records back to the same person — so losing coverage or returning to custody doesn't mean starting over.
The human

Care Promoters

A community health worker who knows a member's name, their plan, and what happened last week. Software isn't the intervention — the relationship is.
The network

Community network

We don't build a parallel system. Astria connects the primary care, behavioral health, housing, transport and referral infrastructure already doing this work.
