Rollout

Standing up one county

The established care model needs a host clinic, a clinical champion, grant funding and a new hire — all at once. That is why it reached 48 sites in twenty years. Astria needs four things a county already has.

One facility

A jail or prison willing to upload what it already has.

One clinic

That takes Medicaid, and takes this population onto a panel.

One Care Promoter

Hired from the population served where possible.

A data agreement

A business associate agreement, patient authorisation, or both.

Sequence

Eight weeks, and what blocks each step

WeekWhat happensWho has to say yesWhat it blocks on
0–2Legal: business associate agreement with the county or its jail health vendor; patient authorisation form drafted and reviewedCounty attorney, jail health vendorThe longest pole. Start it first.
2–4Name the clinic of record and confirm it will accept these patients onto a panelFQHC or clinic leadershipWillingness, not capacity. Some will say no.
3–5Identify who performs the upload inside the facility, and how they are compensated for itSheriff or jail administrator; health vendorFive unpaid minutes is how adoption dies.
4–6Hire and train the Care PromoterAstriaFinding the right person takes longer than the job posting suggests.
5–7Configure state billing rules, the local resource list, and the referral network the state already usesAstriaLibrary work, not custom build.
6–8Go live: first upload, first enrolment, first encounter, first claimEveryone above—
Tiers

Four levels Astria operates at

The same platform at different depths. Each tier makes the next one easier — a county pilot generates the outcome data a health system wants, which generates the volume a state will contract for.

TierBuyerWhat Astria providesWhat it proves
County with no programmeCounty health department, sheriff's office, local reentry councilFull stack: onboarding, matching, a Care Promoter we staff, remote routing where no local provider existsThat a programme can exist where none did
County with infrastructureFQHC, existing reentry programmeOnboarding, matching, the continuity watch, billing captureThat we make an existing programme measurably better
Health systemMulti-county system or FQHC networkMulti-site deployment, cross-county matching, the Astria ID as the linkage layerThat continuity holds when people move between counties
StateState Medicaid agency or managed care planStatewide onboarding, demonstration reporting, the ID as shared infrastructureThat this is infrastructure, not a programme

What we bring

  • The platform and the rules library
  • The Care Promoter, at county tiers
  • Billing capture and demonstration reporting
  • The Astria ID and the continuity watch

What the partner brings

  • Facility access and the upload
  • A clinic willing to accept attribution
  • The legal route for the data
  • Local relationships we do not have

What we never bring

  • A replacement for the existing programme
  • A parallel referral network
  • Data to probation, parole or the courts
  • A promise that an app fixes transport
