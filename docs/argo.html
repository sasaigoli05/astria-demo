What Argo does

Four moves, in order

Any format in

A fax, a scan, a spreadsheet, a discharge record. No schema, no integration, no minimum quality bar.

Extract to structure

Conditions, medications with days of supply, release date, contacts. Every extracted field keeps a link back to the page it came from, so a clinician can check the source.

Assemble coverage

Argo verifies Medicaid status or assembles the application so a certified assister can submit it. Astria does not submit applications itself — assisting with enrolment is a regulated role.

Name the gaps

Missing discharge summary, no contact number, release date unconfirmed. Each becomes a chase task with an owner, rather than an empty field somebody discovers later.

Why this matters commercially

A Michigan state analysis found community health workers spend 8.7 hours a week tracking down resources and making referrals — separate from the 18.5 hours they spend with clients. Across a caseload of twenty-five, that is roughly eighteen hours per client per year on coordination before any care is delivered.

Michigan DHHS / MiCHWA Community Health Worker Caseload Analysis, 2023.

What Argo does not do

It does not contact members directly, make clinical decisions, or appear as a person. It reduces the administrative load — it does not absorb it. Somebody still has to call the housing program and ask whether there is a bed.

Every message that reaches a member is sent by a named human who can be reached back.
