Ordered by risk, not by date

A Care Promoter opens a list sorted by who is closest to falling out of care. Argo has already done the documents, the matching and the notes, so her time goes to the person rather than the paperwork.

Hired from the population served

Where we can, Care Promoters have lived experience of incarceration. This is the design feature with the strongest evidence behind it, and the one we are least willing to compromise.

Reachable where there is no local programme

In person where we have staff on the ground; by phone or video where we don't. Eighteen North Carolina counties have no federally qualified health center — a remote Care Promoter is often the difference between a programme existing and not.

Cadence

The schedule relaxes. It doesn't end.

Contact frequency is set by signals, not by self-report: prescriptions filled, visits attended, coverage active, and whether the clinic confirms the member is on its panel.

Week 1–4

Weekly. Fresh out, and the overdose-risk window is open.

Month 2–6

Monthly. Filling prescriptions, attending visits, answering texts.

Month 7–12

Quarterly. An established patient. We get quiet on purpose.

A signal breaks

Missed refill, ED admission, bounced text, redetermination approaching — back to weekly.

What AI does

Watches the data and decides what matters today. Drafts the message at the right reading level. Fills the forms and writes the encounter note. Prioritises a caseload.

What AI never does

Appear as a person, or sign as one. Originate the relationship. Make a clinical decision. Score a person as a risk. Send anything nobody is accountable for.

The scarce resource is a Care Promoter's attention, not her typing. Automation is there to buy attention back so the real connection happens more often — not fewer times.
