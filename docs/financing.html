What CMS requires

The services are already mandated

Every approved demonstration is bound to a minimum service package. These are not optional extras — they are conditions of the waiver.

Case management

Needs assessment, a care plan, a warm handoff to the post-release care manager, appointment scheduling, and follow-up to ensure the plan is implemented.

Medication-assisted treatment

For substance use disorder as clinically appropriate, with accompanying counselling.

A 30-day medication supply

All prescribed medications, physical and behavioural, in hand at release.

The gap we work in: NASHP found that community health worker claim volume stays low even in states that have explicitly authorised the billing. The barrier is not policy. It is the documentation friction between doing the work and getting paid for it.

The alignment

Revenue follows how long a member stays connected — ours and the site's. We are paid more for doing exactly the thing we exist to do, which is rare enough in health care to be worth stating plainly.

A constraint we observe

We never price as a percentage of claims captured. Contingency arrangements on Medicaid billing are a compliance problem, not a clever business model.

What we have not yet validated

We have not priced this with a payer. The reimbursement rate for a pre-release case management encounter is the single number that decides this model, and obtaining it from a state fee schedule is our immediate next step. Any figure we quote before then is an assumption, and we will say so.

A second open question: in most states community health worker services are billable only when the worker is certified and supervised by an enrolled Medicaid provider. Whether Astria employs Care Promoters directly or contracts through the clinic materially changes the billing path.
